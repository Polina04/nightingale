01 — About

Hospitality with Perspective.

Nightingale Atelier was founded by Yana Soloviova, drawing on more than 20 years of international hospitality experience across hotel and resort operations, guest experience, food and beverage management, pre-opening and hospitality project development.

Our approach is grounded in a belief that successful hospitality begins with understanding a property's individual character, its market and the ambitions of the people behind it.

We combine hands-on operational knowledge with strategic thinking to support owners and developers in making informed decisions throughout the hospitality project lifecycle.

From initial concept development to operational transformation, our focus remains on practical solutions that connect vision, guest experience and long-term commercial value.

Our Mission

To support hospitality owners, investors and developers in creating distinctive and commercially resilient destinations through thoughtful strategy, operational understanding and collaborative project development.

Our Philosophy

People. Places. Possibilities.

We believe successful hospitality is built on a clear vision, genuine service, operational excellence and a strong sense of place.

Every project deserves an individual approach, balancing creativity with commercial discipline and ambition with practical execution.

02 — Strategy & Development

From Vision to Reality.

We provide independent strategic and operational advisory services throughout the development and transformation of hospitality assets.

Our expertise combines concept development, market positioning, operational planning and practical implementation support.

Hospitality Strategy & Positioning

Defining distinctive hospitality concepts, identifying market opportunities and developing positioning strategies aligned with the owner's vision and commercial objectives.

Project & Concept Development

Supporting hospitality projects from initial concept through development planning, operational coordination and implementation.

Hotel Pre-opening & Operational Readiness

Providing practical guidance on operational preparation, service standards, organisational requirements, guest experience and opening strategies.

Hospitality Repositioning & Transformation

Helping owners explore opportunities to transform existing hospitality assets through revised concepts, operating models and strategic positioning.

Operational Advisory

Reviewing operational structures, service delivery and business processes to identify opportunities for improvement and support effective decision-making.

Owner & Operator Advisory

Supporting owners in assessing operating structures, management relationships and strategic alternatives, in collaboration with relevant specialist advisers where required.
